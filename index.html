About the publication

The Loxfeld Strand

The Loxfeld Strand is an independent journal of British affairs created to give student authors a platform to voice their opinions and publish articles on politics, public policy, and national life and culture. We welcome new voices from a range of academic backgrounds and are always looking for authors to share ideas and contribute. The publication began when a group of bored Social Sciences students decided to create an opportunity and an outlet for their own creative thoughts. That informal spirit remains at the heart of what we stand for: serious engagement with public affairs, open to students ready to write.

Editorial principle

The Loxfeld Strand is an independent journal committed to providing informed discourse of British public affairs. The views expressed in commentary and opinion pieces belong to their respective authors and should not be understood as representing the collective position of the publication.

Our contributors come from a range of backgrounds and harbour diverse political perspectives. We welcome disagreement and aim to provide a breadth of coverage on British political life. No individual contributor speaks for the publication as a whole.

Contributors are expected to distinguish factual claims from political argument and analysis, and to make reasonable efforts to ensure that factual assertions are accurate and properly supported. Commentary is interpretive and should be understood as the argument of its author and a cumulative arrangement of source material.
