About the publication

The Loxfeld Strand

The Loxfeld Strand is an independent review created by bored Social Sciences students who simply wanted to write articles. Our aim is to provide informed commentary on foreign and domestic policy on British affairs.

Editorial principle

The views expressed in commentary and opinion pieces are those of their respective authors. Our authors come from various backgrounds and hold various political beliefs. In our goal to present a breadth of British politics, we accept authors of all political persuasions; the view of one author does not dictate the position of The Loxfeld Strand. Factual claims should be presented accurately and distinguished clearly from political opinion and analysis. Opinion pieces and commentary are the creation of the author through research; they are not objective truths.
